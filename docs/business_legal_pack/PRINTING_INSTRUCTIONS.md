# Printing Instructions

Before printing or publishing any pack document:
- confirm branding reads as `Ask Lexi | AI Salon Receptionist` or your final legal trading name
- confirm routes and product references match the current live app (`/`, `/auth`, `/dashboard`, `/legal`)
- confirm billing prices, provider names, and reminder/notification wording are still current
- remove any references to realtime/avatar voice as a live feature unless that paid rollout has actually happened

## Quick Print

1. Open a file in VS Code preview or browser.
2. Print to PDF or paper (`Ctrl+P`).
3. Use A4 paper, portrait orientation.
4. If printing from a rendered markdown preview, enable background graphics where your print dialog supports it.

## Recommended Print Order

1. Print the relevant markdown source documents from `legal/` or `business/`.
2. Export to PDF if you need a solicitor/accountant review pack.
3. Add separate signature pages where needed after legal review.

## Signature Pages

- Add signature blocks where required after legal review.
- Keep one signed master copy and one operational copy.
