# Business + Legal Document Pack

This folder contains the active business and legal documentation pack for Ask Lexi | AI Salon Receptionist.

Important:
- These are professional templates, not legal advice.
- You should have a qualified solicitor/law firm review before publishing or signing.

Current product context this pack should match:
- Public routes: `/`, `/auth`, `/dashboard`, `/legal`
- Current subscriber billing model: Stripe + PayPal with monthly/yearly subscription plans
- Current operator positioning: Ask Lexi as AI receptionist, booking manager, and salon operations workspace
- Current live operational workflows: booking lifecycle, reminder settings, scheduled reminders, notification logging, accounting export, and role-based dashboards
- Paid realtime/avatar voice rollout is intentionally deferred until the end of the build and should not be described as a current live customer feature unless enabled later

## Pack Structure

- `00_MASTER_INDEX.md`: master index and document map.
- `PRINTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md`: how to print or export the markdown documents when needed.
- `legal/`: legal policies and agreements.
- `business/`: business plan and operating documents.

## Included GDPR + Security Coverage

- RoPA template
